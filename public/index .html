Two streams. One platform.

"Sangam" means confluence — the point where separate rivers become one. That's the problem this platform solves: academic syllabi and industry hiring bars have drifted apart, and most institutions have no shared, live view of the gap.

The academic stream

What a university teaches, tracks and certifies.

  • Curriculum mapped against live semester credits and subjects
  • Faculty proposals for workshops, hackathons and research labs
  • Institutional readiness scoring across full engineering cohorts
  • Verified coursework and project credentials per student

The industry stream

What companies actually hire and pay for, in real time.

  • Live job requirements benchmarked against verified skills
  • AI-ranked candidate pipelines tied to real competencies
  • Sponsored FDPs, grants and consultancy work for faculty
  • Transparent lifecycle status from applied to selected

Where these two meet is the readiness score — a single number every role can act on.

A closer look at what makes readiness measurable.

Four features that turn "the skills gap" from a talking point into a live, testable number.

Student · Career Quest Map

Milestones that update your readiness the moment you clear them.

Each sprint is a real competency — not a checkbox. Clearing Docker or Kubernetes recalculates your readiness score immediately, and a targeted micro-test on any weak skill can push it further with a live boost.

  • 5 sprint milestones per roadmap, seeded from real hiring mandates
  • Adaptive reassessment on weak skills, e.g. +9% on passing a Docker & caching quiz
  • Canvas confetti and XP feedback on every milestone clear

College · Curriculum gap diagnostics

Find the exact syllabus gaps before students do.

Sangam compares live technology demand from active postings against your current curriculum and flags the deficit, by subject, before it shows up as low placement numbers next year.

  • Named, ranked gaps — not a generic "modernize the curriculum" note
  • Tied directly to the same benchmarks used in student readiness scores
  • One-click routing into faculty FDP recommendations that close each gap
